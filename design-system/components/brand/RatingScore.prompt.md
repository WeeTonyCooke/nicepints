Compact rating pill — translucent blurred backdrop, numeral coloured by band (gold ≥9, amber ≥8, copper ≥7, stone below). Sits over a photo, e.g. on Find-a-Pint result cards. Shows `/10`.

```jsx
<RatingScore score={8.4} size="md" />
<RatingScore score={9.2} size="lg" showMax={false} />
```

For the dominant hero/feed score use `EditorialRatingBlock` instead.
