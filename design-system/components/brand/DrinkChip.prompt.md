Drink-category chip — the single place the drink-type accent colour is allowed (per DESIGN-PRINCIPLES §6). A 3px left border + text in the accent, on a graphite chip. Makes the feed scannable by drink at a glance.

```jsx
<DrinkChip slug="guinness">Guinness</DrinkChip>
<DrinkChip slug="guinness-00">Guinness 0.0 · Draught</DrinkChip>
<DrinkChip slug="murphys">Murphy's</DrinkChip>
```

Slugs: guinness (cream) · guinness-00 (slate) · beamish (red) · murphys (plum) · other (muted). Never put the accent on backgrounds, card glows, or photo scrims.
